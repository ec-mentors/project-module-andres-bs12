Privacy Policy

NutritionTracker ("the App") helps users log meals and track nutrition goals.

Information we collect

  • Google account profile data you authorize at sign-in (name, email, profile picture).
  • Nutrition data you enter (meals, macros, goals, favorites).
  • Optional Telegram pairing token if you connect the Telegram bot.

How we use information

  • Authenticate you and associate your data with your account.
  • Display dashboards, goals, and meal history.
  • Process AI-assisted meal parsing when you use Nutria features.

Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In for authentication.
  • OpenAI for optional AI meal analysis (when enabled).
  • Telegram if you use the optional bot integration.
  • AWS for application hosting and database storage.

Data retention & security

Data is stored in a secured PostgreSQL database. We do not sell personal data.
